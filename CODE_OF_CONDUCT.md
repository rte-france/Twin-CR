# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make the **Twin-CR** community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and contributions of all individuals, regardless of characteristics including race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status. The same privileges of participation are extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant.

---

## Encouraged Behaviors

While acknowledging differences in social norms, we all strive to meet our community's expectations for positive behavior. We also understand that our words and actions may be interpreted differently than we intend based on culture, background, or native language.

With these considerations in mind, we agree to behave mindfully toward each other and act in ways that center our shared values, including:

- **Respecting the project mission:** Honoring the purpose of twin-cr, our technical discussions, and our collaborative workflows.
- **Engaging kindly and honestly:** Fostering mutual respect across issues, PR reviews, and chat channels.
- **Valuing diverse viewpoints:** Listening to alternate technical approaches and differing life experiences.
- **Taking ownership:** Taking responsibility for our actions, communications, and code contributions.
- **Feedback literacy:** Gracefully giving and accepting constructive, technical feedback.
- **Repairing harm:** Actively committing to de-escalate tensions and repair harm when misunderstandings or missteps occur.
- **Community well-being:** Behaving in ways that sustain the long-term health and growth of the twin-cr community.

---

## Restricted Behaviors

Instances, threats, or promotion of the following behaviors are direct violations of this Code of Conduct:

- **Harassment:** Violating explicitly expressed boundaries or continuing personal attention after a clear request to stop.
- **Character attacks:** Making insulting, demeaning, or pejorative remarks directed at an individual or group.
- **Stereotyping or discrimination:** Judging or characterizing anyone’s personality, code, or capabilities based on immutable identities or demographic traits.
- **Sexualization:** Displaying sexualized language or imagery, or engaging in unwelcome romantic or intimate advances.
- **Violating confidentiality:** Doxxing or sharing personal details (e.g., real names, physical addresses, private emails) without explicit consent.
- **Endangerment:** Inciting, threatening, or encouraging violence, self-harm, or other physical/psychological harm.
- **Destructive behavior:** Any conduct calculated to derail, intimidate, or compromise the safety of twin-cr participants.

### Other Prohibited Conduct

- **Misleading identity:** Impersonating another contributor, maintainer, or third party, or creating alternative identities to evade moderation.
- **Plagiarism & intellectual theft:** Failing to properly credit or license sources of contributed code, documentation, or assets.
- **Spam & unsolicited promotion:** Posting unsolicited advertisements, token sales, commercial promotions, or irrelevant links outside established channels.
- **Irresponsible communication:** Knowingly linking to, embedding, or amplifying restricted content.

## Reporting an Issue

Tensions can occur between contributors even during honest technical disagreements. Not every conflict represents a Code of Conduct violation; however, when an incident occurs, report it as soon as possible.

- **Escalation Path:** If the report involves a specific maintainer, reach out directly to another core maintainer via GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting or direct message.

### What to include in a report

1. A summary of the incident and links/screenshots of the relevant commits, PRs, comments, or chat messages.
2. The usernames or accounts of the individuals involved.
3. Any prior context, previous warnings, or attempts made to de-escalate the issue.

### Our commitment

Community moderators will acknowledge receipt within **48 hours**, review logs and context impartially, and keep proceedings confidential. Enforcement measures are communicated privately to the involved parties, with public summaries issued only when necessary for community transparency or safety.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

When an investigation determines that this Code of Conduct has been violated, the maintainers will apply the following enforcement ladder based on impact and severity. Lower steps may be bypassed for severe violations.

### 1. Warning

- **Trigger:** A single minor violation or initial unconstructive interaction.
- **Consequence:** A private, written warning outlining the violation and expected behavioral adjustments.
- **Resolution / Repair:** A written acknowledgment of responsibility and a commitment to avoid repeating the behavior.

### 2. Temporarily Limited Activities (Cooldown)

- **Trigger:** Repeated minor offenses or a first moderate incident (e.g., escalating hostility in PR reviews).
- **Consequence:** A mandatory cooldown period restricting access to specific channels, issues, or direct interactions with affected individuals.
- **Resolution / Repair:** Reflection during the cooldown, an apology to impacted parties where appropriate, and respectful re-entry into community discussions.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

- **Trigger:** Persistent refusal to follow guidelines despite warnings, or a single severe infraction.
- **Consequence:** Complete suspension from all twin-cr repositories, issue trackers, and linked spaces for a set timeframe (e.g., 14 to 30 days).
- **Resolution / Repair:** Demonstration of accountability and agreement to clear conditions before privileges are reinstated.

### 4. Permanent Ban

- **Trigger:** Sustained patterns of abusive behavior, serious harassment, hate speech, doxxing, or safety endangerment.
- **Consequence:** Irrevocable termination of access to all twin-cr GitHub repositories, discussions, and associated communication platforms.
- **Resolution / Repair:** No remediation is possible at this level.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies across all official **Twin-CR** environments:

- Repositories, issues, discussions, pull requests, and commit histories.
- Official community chat spaces (Discord, Slack, Matrix, or IRC).
- Public or virtual spaces where an individual officially represents the **Twin-CR** project (e.g., conferences, technical talks, social media statements).
